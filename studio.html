How to play Tap any key you can see to put a tentacle there; it stays. Tap a tentacle to lift it, then tap where it goes. Each placement is a turn: everything you have seen moves, then the tank opens around the new tentacle. Keep at least one key held at all times or you let go (unless no holding is ticked). G+. starts, restarts, and after a run takes you where the tank sends you. Die, and the tank sends you to another one you have not cleared.
