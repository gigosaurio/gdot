Hold G and tap . to start. Each tank starts on the key it is named after. Tap any key you can see to put a tentacle there; it stays. Tap a tentacle to lift it. Every placement is a turn: everything you have seen moves first, then your tentacle lands. Put enough tentacles down (the tray under the tank), on every starfish you find, to clear the tank. Keep at least one key held down or you let go (tick no holding to turn that off).
