Hold G and tap . to start. Each tank starts on the key it is named after. Tap a key next to one of your tentacles to put a tentacle there; it stays. Every tap is a turn: everything you have seen moves first (its arrow shows where), then your tentacle lands.

Fill every open key in as few taps as you can: the tray counts them, and par (top right) is the fewest. A creature that lands on your tentacle takes that key until it leaves; you only lose when it takes them all. Tap a tentacle to lift it (free); lift and put back to wait a turn. Hold a starfish and the algae around it becomes more water to fill. Some tanks give you ink: tap Space and a cloud around your newest tentacle keeps creatures out for two presses. Keep at least one key held down or you let go (tick no holding to turn that off). In some later zones a bite still ends the run.
