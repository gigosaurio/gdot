Nothing is hidden. Creatures swim fixed rounds and ignore you. Every press is one tick: everything moves, then your arm lands. So a key is open when nothing will be on it after the move, even if something sits on it now: you fill the wake. A creature that swims onto an arm takes it (the key is free again once it leaves). The strip at the bottom of each key is its next five ticks: red when something will be on it; the first slot is "can I land here now". You can only reach keys next to an arm. Space waits one tick. You win the moment every key is an arm or a creature. Fewest presses wins; par is the fewest possible.
